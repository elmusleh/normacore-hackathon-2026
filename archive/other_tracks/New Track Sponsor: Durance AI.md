Builders,

We’re excited to welcome Durance AI as a Track Sponsor of the 2nd edition of the Berlin Robotics & AI Hackathon!

Durance AI is a French deeptech startup building brain-inspired AI chips for robots and drones. Their technology lets autonomous systems see and understand their surroundings using a fraction of the energy, so they can run longer on a single battery.

Durance AI will have their own track at the hackathon. We’ll share all the details soon, so stay tuned.

And they’re hiring! They are looking for:

    Robotics and drone engineers with a sense of Machine Learning

    People with a hacker, maker, pirate mindset

    Junior, senior and lead positions

A big thank you to Edgar Lemaire and the whole Durance AI team for supporting the next generation of builders.

November 21-22, Berlin
10.000€ in prizes: cash, credits, robots and more
