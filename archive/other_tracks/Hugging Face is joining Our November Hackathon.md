🤗 Hugging Face is joining Our November Hackathon
Hack lab
Hack lab

Oct 28, 2026

•

1 min read

We’re excited to announce that Hugging Face will host a dedicated track at our robotics & AI hackathon on November 21–22 in Berlin.

🦾 The track: participants will build with the SO-101, Hugging Face’s open-source robotic arm, and use LeRobot to train and deploy real-world projects over one weekend.

A huge thank you to Nikodem Bartnik for his support in making this happen. 

Got an SO-101? If you own one and can bring it to the event, let us know in the comments. More arms means more teams building!

⚡ Spots are limited. Registration is open now, so apply as soon as possible to secure your place.

👉 Register here, they will close soon: https://luma.com/h0mcaqio

See you in Berlin! 🇩🇪
