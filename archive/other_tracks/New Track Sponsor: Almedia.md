We’re excited to welcome Almedia as a Track Sponsor of the Berlin Robotics & AI Hackathon!

Almedia is one of Europe’s fastest-growing companies: a Berlin-based adtech company that rewards a community of 50M+ users for engaging with brands through Freecash.

They’re currently hiring for two senior roles:

    Machine Learning Engineer: engineers who build and ship production ML end to end, across ranking, recommendations, bidding and offer/payout optimisation.

    Data Scientist: strong in experimentation and causal inference, with modelling that drives business decisions.

They’re also open to junior talent with similar aptitude and will tailor roles to the right candidate. If this sounds like you, join their track at the hackathon!

Thank you Almedia for supporting the event! 🤖

Overview

Almedia is defining the new standard in the $1T advertising market by rewarding user engagement. We believe that the user’s time and attention should be a compensated asset, not an exploited resource, making advertising better for both sides. To achieve this, we build the world’s most intelligent platform to discover and engage with games and brands. According to the Financial Times’ FT1000, Almedia is already the fastest-growing advertising company in Europe.

Our gamified reward system motivates users to try games or apps, but unlike traditional UA, our players continue engaging long term. Consumers receive rewards for purchases via cashback, while gamers are rewarded for completing levels with real-money rewards. Through Almedia products, app developers and brands are able to acquire new customers, promote customer loyalty, and strengthen their brand.

Beyond user acquisition, our monetization solution, Link, enables publishers to generate incremental revenue from high-intent audiences, increasing ARPU and lifetime value without compromising user experience.

With a growing user base of over 80 million users, our products Freecash.com and Link drive unparalleled returns to over 1200 advertisers through our data-driven, rewarded User Acquisition approach. For the first time, consumers receive rewards purely for interacting with advertising and spending time on products, such as games and apps. With a 4.6* rating from over 300,000 reviews on Trustpilot, Almedia products, notably Freecash, are trusted by advertisers and consumers alike.
