Builders,

We’re excited to welcome NormaCore as a Track Sponsor of the 2nd edition of the Berlin Robotics & AI Hackathon!

NormaCore is building the open-source data layer for Physical AI. Their runtime, Station, unifies fragmented robot SDKs into a single interface and captures high-fidelity data across any robot fleet.

NormaCore will run its own track, built around ElRobot: their 3D-printable 7+1 DOF arm designed for teleoperation and demonstration recording. Participants will get hands-on with the arms to collect data, train policies and build real-world manipulation projects.

A big thank you to the NormaCore team for supporting the next generation of builders.
